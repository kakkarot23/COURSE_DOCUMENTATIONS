51) What is Problem Definition?
Problem definition is the process of clearly describing and understanding the issue or question that the research aims to address. It ensures that the researcher focuses on the key aspects of the problem without unnecessary distractions.

52) What is the purpose of Problem Definition?
The purpose of problem definition is to clarify the research objectives and provide a clear direction for the study. It helps establish what needs to be studied and sets the foundation for a successful research process.

53) What are the characteristics of a good Problem Definition?
A good problem definition is specific and measurable, meaning it clearly defines the issue and allows progress to be tracked. It should also be concise, relevant, and aligned with the overall research goals.

54) What is Research Design?
Research design is a detailed plan or framework for conducting research, guiding the researcher on how to collect and analyze data. It ensures the research is systematic and yields reliable and valid results.

55) What are the types of Research Design?
The main types of research design include experimental and quasi-experimental. Experimental research involves manipulating variables to observe their effects, while quasi-experimental research examines cause-and-effect relationships without full control over variables.
